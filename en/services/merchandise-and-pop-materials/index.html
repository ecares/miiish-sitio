What is it about?

Merchandise means products with your brand: T-shirts, mugs, bags, caps or anything you can imagine. They're great as gifts for customers, uniforms for your team or even as products to sell, and they take your brand out into the street.

POP materials are everything that helps sell at the point of sale: posters, hanging signs, displays, window stickers or counter stands. They are what makes someone notice your product among many others.

Tap the underlined words and we will explain them simply.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • You're going to a trade show or event and have no branded materials.
  • Your product gets lost on the shelf among competitors.
  • You want to give customers something that won't end up in a drawer.
  • Your shop doesn't convey the same image as your social media.

Frequently asked questions

Do you manufacture the products?

We handle the design and get everything ready for production. If you want, we recommend suppliers and coordinate with them.

Can I see what it will look like before production?

Yes. We show you each piece as a mockup or 3D render to avoid surprises.

Do you do small quantities?

The design is the same for a few units or many. We help you choose techniques and suppliers that make sense for the quantity.

Does my brand need to be ready?

It helps. If you don't have a defined identity yet, we can start with branding.

Shall we talk about your project?

Tell us what you need and we will reply within one business day.
