What is it about?

Branding is your business identity. It is not just the logo: it is the name, the colors, the way you talk and the feeling your brand leaves when someone sees it on Instagram, on a bag or on your shop door.

When a brand is well made, people recognize it without reading the name, trust it more and are willing to pay what it is worth. When every piece looks different, customers get confused and your business looks smaller than it is.

Tap the underlined words and we will explain them simply.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • A friend made your logo, or you did it yourself, and it no longer represents you.
  • Every post, poster or label looks different from the last one.
  • You are launching a new business or product and do not know what to call it.
  • Your competitors look more professional, even if your product is just as good or better.

How we help you

  1. 01

    Knowing who you talk to

    We talk about your business, your customers and your competitors to define your positioning: what makes you different and why someone should choose you.

  2. 02

    Finding the name

    If you are starting from scratch or want a change, we do naming: we suggest easy-to-remember names and check that the web domain and social handles are available.

  3. 03

    Designing how you look

    We create your visual identity: logo, versions for light and dark backgrounds, colors, fonts and icons that work together.

  4. 04

    Putting it in writing

    We put together a simple brand guidelines document so you, your team or any supplier always use the brand the same way.

  5. 05

    Bringing it to your products

    We design packaging: containers, labels and boxes that look good on the shelf and in photos.

How we work

  1. 1

    We talk

    In the first meeting you tell us about your business, what you like, what you don't and where you want to go.

  2. 2

    We research and propose

    We look at your competitors and show you possible directions, with references, so we can choose one together.

  3. 3

    We design and adjust

    We develop the chosen direction and refine it with your feedback until you feel it represents you.

  4. 4

    We hand everything over

    You get files ready for print and social media, plus the guide to use them.

Frequently asked questions

Can I refresh my logo without changing the whole brand?

Yes. We can do an update: keep what people already recognize and modernize the rest, so you don't lose your current customers.

What if I don't like the first proposal?

Adjusting is normal. Before designing we agree on a direction with references, and then we do rounds of changes based on your feedback.

Do I own the files?

Yes. When we finish we hand over all the final files so you can use them wherever you want.

Do you also do social media and the website with the new brand?

Yes. Since we are a full team, we can bring your brand to your social media, website, videos and printed materials.
