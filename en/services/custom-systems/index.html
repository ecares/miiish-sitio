Organize your business with a system made for you

What is it about?

Many businesses grow using Excel spreadsheets, notebooks and chats. It works at first, but at some point orders get lost, stock doesn't add up and nobody knows how much was really sold.

A custom system is a program designed exactly for the way you work: you don't have to adapt to generic software full of features you don't use. It can also talk to your online store, invoicing or other tools through APIs, so information moves on its own.

Tap the underlined words and we will explain them simply.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • You use several spreadsheets that never match each other.
  • You sell in-store and online and your stock gets out of sync.
  • You waste time copying data from one place to another.
  • You can't easily see how much you sold, what moves most or who your best customers are.

Frequently asked questions

Why not use existing software?

Sometimes that's the better choice, and we'll tell you. A custom system makes sense when your way of working doesn't fit generic programs or when you need to connect several tools.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use it?

No. We design it to be simple and train you and your team to use it.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use password-protected users, per-person permissions and data backups.

Can it connect to my online store?

Yes. We connect it to Shopify, Jumpseller, WooCommerce and other platforms through APIs.
