What is it about?

Animation is great for telling things that are hard to film: how an app works, a service you can't see, a process inside something or an idea. With drawings and moving graphics, complicated things are understood quickly and remembered better.

A brand mascot or character puts a face on your business. It can appear on your social media, packaging, ads and videos, and it makes people recognize you and feel fond of you.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Your product or service is hard to explain in a few words.
  • Your logo is static and you want it to look modern in videos and on social media.
  • You want your brand to have a friendlier, more fun personality.
  • You need eye-catching content, but have nothing physical to film.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between 2D animation and motion graphics?

2D animation brings drawn characters to life, like cartoons. Motion graphics animate text, icons and shapes to explain information.

Do I own the mascot?

Yes. When we finish we hand over the character files so you can use it across your brand.

Can you add a voice-over?

Yes. We can add voice-over in Spanish, English or Portuguese, plus subtitles.

Does animation work for ads?

Yes. Animation grabs attention on social media and works very well in digital advertising.
