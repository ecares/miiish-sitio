What is it about?

Video is the most watched format on social media and the one that best explains a product in a few seconds. A good video shows how what you sell is used, who is behind it and why it is worth it.

Producing a video is not just hitting "record": you need to think about what to say, how to say it in the first three seconds, shoot with good light and sound, and edit so it looks professional on a phone. That is what we do.

Tap the underlined words and we will explain them simply.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • You know you need videos, but don't know where to start.
  • You film with your phone and it doesn't look like the brands you admire.
  • Your still-image ads are not working as well anymore.
  • You need a video that explains your company to customers or investors.

How we help you

  1. 01

    The idea and the script

    We figure out what to say and how, and draw it as a storyboard so you can see the video before we shoot it.

  2. 02

    The shoot

    We film at your shop, outdoors or in a studio, with professional cameras, lighting and sound, and with cinematography.

  3. 03

    The edit

    We edit with rhythm, add music, text and subtitles, and adapt the video to each network: vertical, square or horizontal.

  4. 04

    Color and sound

    We do color grading so everything looks like your brand, and we clean up and mix the audio.

  5. 05

    Ad versions

    We prepare cuts of different lengths to use in digital advertising and compare which one works best.

How we work

  1. 1

    We define the idea

    We talk about what you want the video to achieve, where it will be seen and who it is for.

  2. 2

    We prepare the shoot

    We write the script, build the storyboard and coordinate location, people and date.

  3. 3

    We shoot

    We bring the gear needed and film everything planned, plus extra shots for social media.

  4. 4

    We edit and deliver

    We edit, show you a first version, make the adjustments and deliver the final files.

Frequently asked questions

Do you shoot outside Santiago?

Yes. We can arrange shoots in other cities; we discuss it depending on the project.

Do I need actors or models?

It depends on the idea. Often it works better to show your own team; if actors are needed, we help you find them.

Can I ask for changes to the edit?

Yes. We show you a first version and make the agreed adjustments before delivery.

Do I get the video for each social network?

Yes. We deliver vertical, square and horizontal versions depending on where you will publish it.

Shall we talk about your project?

Tell us what you need and we will reply within one business day.
