Service 07 · 3D modeling & rendering

Show your product before it is made

We build your product, packaging, furniture or space in 3D and create images and animations so real they look like photos, with no photo shoot needed.

Get a quote for this →See what it includes ↓

What is it about?

3D is like building your product inside the computer. Once it exists digitally, we can show it from any angle, in any color, in any setting and with perfect lighting.

This is useful to sell something that isn't manufactured yet, test packaging variations before printing, show what a space will look like or get catalog images without relying on photos. And if you later change a detail, you don't have to reshoot everything.

Tap the underlined words and we will explain them simply.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • You want to launch a pre-sale, but the product doesn't exist yet.
  • Your product photos don't look as good as you'd like or are expensive to redo.
  • You need to show many color or model variations.
  • You have to present a booth, shop or space that isn't built yet.

How we help you

  1. 01

    Your product in 3D

    We do 3D modeling of your product from blueprints, photos or an idea.

  2. 02

    Realistic materials

    With texturing we give each surface its real look: metal, glass, fabric, wood or plastic.

  3. 03

    Images that look like photos

    We create photorealistic renders of your product on white or in settings, ready for your online store and ads.

  4. 04

    Packaging in 3D

    We test your packaging in 3D to see how it will look on the shelf before printing it.

  5. 05

    3D animations

    We animate your product spinning, assembling or showing its parts, for social media, ads and presentations.

How we work

  1. 1

    We gather information

    You send us blueprints, measurements, photos, samples or references of what you want to show.

  2. 2

    We model

    We build the 3D model and send you simple views to confirm shapes and proportions.

  3. 3

    We set the scene and render

    We apply materials, light and setting, and generate the images or animation.

  4. 4

    We adjust and deliver

    We make the agreed changes and deliver high-quality files.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to get started?

Blueprints or measurements are ideal, but we can also work from photos, a physical sample or a sketch.

Can you tell it's not a photo?

A good render is very hard to tell apart from a photo. We take care of materials, light and details so it looks real.

Can I reuse the model for other images?

Yes. Once the model exists, creating new images, colors or settings is faster.

Is it useful for my online store?

Yes. Many stores use renders instead of photos because all images look consistent and are easy to change.
