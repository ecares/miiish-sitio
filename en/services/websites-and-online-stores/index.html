What is it about?

Your website is the only place on the internet that is completely yours. Social networks change their rules, but your site is always there to explain who you are, show what you sell and take orders or inquiries at any time.

An online store, or e-commerce, goes one step further: customers choose the product, pay by card or transfer and you just prepare the shipment. And to know if it is working, we set it up to measure visits and sales from day one.

?If you don't understand a term, click the underlined text and we'll explain it in plain words.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Your website is old, slow or looks bad on a phone.
  • You sell through Instagram or WhatsApp and waste time answering prices and payment details.
  • When people search for your business on Google you don't show up, or your competitor does.
  • You don't know how many people visit your site or where they come from.

Frequently asked questions

Which platform is right for me?

It depends on what you sell and how much you want to grow. Shopify and Jumpseller are very easy to manage; WooCommerce and WordPress give more freedom. We recommend the best option once we know your business.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes. We give you a dashboard to change text, photos, prices and products, and we show you how to use it.

Can I take local card payments?

Yes. We connect payment providers such as Mercado Pago, Webpay or Flow, depending on the platform.

How long until it shows up on Google?

Google takes some time to review a new site. We prepare it properly and connect it to Search Console to speed up that process.
