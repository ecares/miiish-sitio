What is it about?

Today many people discover a brand on Instagram or TikTok before they see its website or shop. If your accounts look abandoned or improvised, that customer moves on to another one.

Managing social media is not just posting photos: it means deciding what to say each week, keeping a recognizable style, replying on time and checking what works. Keep in mind: lots of followers or likes do not mean more sales. That is why we measure what matters: messages, inquiries and visits to your store.

Tap the underlined words and we will explain them simply.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • You post when you remember, and then weeks go by with nothing.
  • You don't know what to post and end up repeating the same thing.
  • People send you direct messages and nobody has time to answer.
  • Your posts don't look like each other or like your brand.

Frequently asked questions

Which networks do you work with?

Mainly Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn. We recommend where to be based on who your customers are; you don't need to be everywhere.

Do I approve every post?

Yes. Before publishing we send you the calendar and designs so you can review them and ask for changes.

Will this make me sell more?

Well-kept accounts help people trust you and reach out. If you want to reach new people faster, the best move is to add digital advertising.

Do I need my own photos?

It helps a lot. If you don't have them, we can do a photo shoot or film your products and your team.

Shall we talk about your project?

Tell us what you need and we will reply within one business day.
