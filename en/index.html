FAQ

What people ask us

?If you don't understand a term, click the underlined text and we'll explain it in plain words.

What is ¡Miiish!?

+

We are an agency from Santiago, Chile that helps businesses and startups look good and sell more. We do branding, run your social media, build your website or e-commerce store, produce videos, motion graphics and 3D modeling, and run paid media on Instagram, Facebook, Google and TikTok. All with one team.

Do you only work with companies in Chile?

+

No. We are based in Santiago and work remotely with brands across Chile and abroad. Meetings can be in person or by video call, in Spanish, English or Portuguese.

Do you run ads on Instagram, Facebook and Google?

+

Yes. We create the ads, set the targeting and run A/B testing to see what works best. Every month we show you, with simple numbers, your conversions, your CPA and your ROAS.

Do you build online stores and custom systems?

+

Yes. We build e-commerce stores where customers buy and pay directly (on Shopify, Jumpseller or WooCommerce) and WordPress websites. If you keep sales, stock or customers in spreadsheets, we build a custom system with CRM, integrations and automations.

What is branding and what does it include?

+

Branding is your brand identity: its name, how it looks and how it talks. We start with your positioning, continue with naming if needed and the visual identity (logo, colors and fonts), and hand over brand guidelines with every file ready.

Can you refresh my brand without starting from scratch?

+

Yes. If your brand is already known, we update it without your customers losing track of it: we modernize the logo, tidy up colors and fonts, and refresh your designs. If you need a bigger change, we propose a new brand.

What kind of 3D work do you do?

+

We do 3D modeling, texturing and rendering of products, packaging, furniture and spaces, plus 3D animations for ads, social media and online stores. It lets you show a product before it is made, launch a pre-sale or get perfect images without a photo shoot.

Do I need the actual product to get 3D images?

+

No. We can create it from drawings, measurements, sketches or reference photos. We show you progress so you can adjust shapes, materials and colors before the final image.

I'm not sure what I need. Can you help?

+

Of course. Many of our clients arrive with just an idea. Tell us what you sell and what you would like to achieve, and we will recommend where to start, no strings attached.

How do we get started?
